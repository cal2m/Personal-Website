Shelf
Stabilizer.

A small mechanical component designed to help prevent camper-van storage shelves from rattling and shifting vertically over bumps.

01 / THE PROBLEM

A shelf that moved on the road

The rear-door storage cabinet shelves could rattle and shift vertically when the van travelled over bumps. The design needed to work with the existing MOLLE panels and be simple to fit and use.

02 / DESIGN APPROACH

Twist, fit, and hold

I used Onshape to develop a twist-and-fit stabilizer that worked with the existing panel geometry. The goal was to create a practical part that could be installed without redesigning the surrounding storage system.

03 / ITERATION

Fit was only the first requirement

I made roughly four to five versions. After establishing the fit, I refined usability, including a larger grip handle, symmetric orientation, and a tight, satisfying click. Each change addressed how the part would actually be handled, not just how it looked in CAD.

04 / OUTCOME & REFLECTION

From prototype to product

The stabilizer was shipped to existing customers and incorporated into new builds. This project taught me to evaluate a component not only by whether it fits, but by how intuitively it installs and works in its real environment.
