CASE STUDY / 02 · MECHANICAL DESIGN

Shelf
Stabilizer.

A small mechanical component designed to help prevent camper-van storage shelves from rattling and shifting vertically over bumps.

ROLEEngineering Intern
CONTEXTCamper van storage
FOCUSOnshape · 3D printing · iteration
PROJECT OVERVIEW

Add a clear photo of the finished stabilizer installed or beside the shelf.

01 / CHALLENGE

A shelf that moved on the road

The rear-door storage cabinet shelves could rattle and shift vertically when the van travelled over bumps. The design needed to work with the existing MOLLE panels and be simple to fit and use.

02 / APPROACH

Twist, fit, and hold

I used Onshape to develop a twist-and-fit stabilizer that worked with the existing panel geometry. The goal was a practical part that could be installed without redesigning the surrounding storage system.

CAD / DESIGN

Add the Onshape model, key dimensions, and how the part engages with the panel. Remove confidential details first.

03 / MY ROLE

Iterate for fit and usability

I made roughly four to five versions. After establishing the fit, I refined usability with a larger grip handle, symmetric orientation, and a tight, satisfying click. Each change addressed how the part would actually be handled, not just how it looked in CAD.

04 / OUTCOME & LEARNING

From prototype to product

The stabilizer was shipped to existing customers and incorporated into new builds. The project taught me to evaluate a component by how intuitively it installs and works in its real environment, as well as whether it fits.
