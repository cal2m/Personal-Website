01 / THE CONSTRAINT

When the available hardware changes the design

During fabrication, a gearbox assembly was too long for the bolt lengths available. The design needed to be adjusted so the assembly could be secured using the hardware on hand.

02 / MY DECISION

Rework the geometry

I shortened the gearbox, changing the shaft and gear spacing so the shorter available bolts could be used. This was a practical trade-off between the intended geometry and the constraints of the actual build.

03 / WHAT I LEARNED

Design for the real assembly

The experience reinforced that a mechanism is not just a CAD model. Fastener access, part spacing, available hardware, fabrication, and assembly all affect whether a design can be built successfully.
