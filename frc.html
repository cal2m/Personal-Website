01 / CHALLENGE

When available hardware changes the design

During fabrication, a gearbox assembly was too long for the bolt lengths available. The assembly needed to be adjusted so it could be secured with the hardware on hand.

02 / APPROACH

Rework the geometry

I shortened the gearbox, changing the shaft and gear spacing so the shorter available bolts could be used. This was a practical trade-off between the intended geometry and the constraints of the actual build.

03 / MY ROLE

Contribute through fabrication and assembly

As Build Department Lead, I contributed to the robot build and worked through the gearbox adjustment as a practical assembly problem. Fastener access, part spacing, available hardware, and fabrication all affected the design decision.

04 / OUTCOME & LEARNING

Design for the real assembly

The experience reinforced that a mechanism is more than a CAD model. A successful design also has to account for how parts can be made, accessed, fastened, and assembled with the resources available.
