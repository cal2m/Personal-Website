CASE STUDY / 01 · ENERGY SYSTEMS

Smartlet
DC Outlet.

A student innovation project exploring whether distributing DC power more directly could reduce repeated AC-to-DC conversion for electronic devices.

ROLECo-founder · Finance & research
CONTEXTFIRST LEGO League innovation project
FOCUSResearch · Planning · Product concept

01 / CHALLENGE

Where is energy converted?

Research into how electricity is distributed and used led our team to the losses involved when AC electricity is converted to DC for many electronic devices. We asked whether a DC-based outlet system could address part of that problem.

02 / APPROACH

Explore a different outlet experience

The proposed Smartlet concept combined a magnetic connection with a priority-level system intended to help manage power during shortages. These were design goals and proposed features, not proof that the full system had been implemented or validated.

03 / MY ROLE

Research beyond the initial idea

My work included financial modeling, patent research, business planning, marketing, and product development. I researched existing outlets and related products and contacted companies for industry advice. I also considered compatibility, component packaging, adoption, and practical implementation.

04 / OUTCOME & LEARNING

Questions for the next iteration

Important open questions include supporting different voltage requirements, fitting the necessary components into a standard outlet, and validating whether the concept delivers the intended benefits. The project taught me to connect an exciting idea with the research and constraints needed to assess it.
