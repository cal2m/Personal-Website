DC Outlet.

01 / THE QUESTION

Where is energy converted?

Our FIRST LEGO League innovation project explored the energy journey. Researching how electricity is distributed and used led me to the losses involved when AC electricity is converted to DC for many electronic devices. We asked whether a DC-based outlet system could address part of that problem.

02 / THE CONCEPT

A different outlet experience

The proposed Smartlet concept combined a magnetic connection with a priority-level system intended to help manage power during shortages. These were design goals and proposed features, not proof that the full system had been implemented or validated.

03 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Research beyond the initial idea

My work included financial modeling, patent research, business planning, marketing, and product development. I researched existing outlets and related products and contacted companies for industry advice. The project pushed me to consider not only the concept, but also compatibility, component packaging, adoption, and practical implementation.

04 / LIMITATIONS & NEXT QUESTIONS

What still needs solving?

Important open questions include supporting different voltage requirements, fitting the necessary components into a standard outlet, and validating whether the concept delivers the intended benefits. These are next steps rather than completed results.
